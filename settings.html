Chart Retuner

Remaps every chart onto your real instrument's tuning (bass and guitar profiles, customizable below).

Turn off to play every chart in its original tuning without uninstalling the plugin. Also available as a quick toggle in the player controls, mid-song.

Target Tunings

Every chart is remapped onto a target tuning — any pitches, 4-8 strings. Each arrangement class (bass / rhythm / lead) has its own profile below, and all three pick from the same pool: your saved custom tunings listed first, then the built-in presets below a divider. Picking either opens it for editing below. Any profile may use any tuning — a bass tuning on a guitar arrangement is fine. Add your own for AEADG, BbEbAbDbGb, a 7-string, or anything else. Strings can only be added/removed from the ends, never the middle; a new string gets a suggested note that you can change.

Apply to the highway immediately — no reload required.

Edit tuning

Picking any tuning above — built-in or custom — opens it here. Your edits apply immediately as one session-only live preview across bass, rhythm, and lead; they do not modify any selected or saved profile. The preview is discarded on Cancel, Save, selecting another tuning, or restarting the plugin. Save is disabled until you change something. Editing a built-in preset names it “New custom preset N” and always saves as a new custom tuning — built-ins themselves can't be overwritten or deleted, and no tuning can be saved under a built-in's name. Editing a custom tuning keeps its name and updates it in place; Delete removes it.

Highest fret this tuning remaps onto. Most charts never go past fret 20 — raise it for extended-range instruments or the occasional high transcribed solo, lower it for a genuinely short-necked instrument.

A capo on the tuning below you're building — independent of any capo the chart itself was recorded with. Off by default: play the tuning as-is unless you turn this on.

Clamp a capo on top of the string pitches below — one fret = one half-step up per string, and the frets above (max fret − capo) fall off the end of the neck. The player controls' Capo pill can switch it on/off quickly; the fret itself is set here.

Shift every chart up/down whole octaves before remapping — no key change involved. +1 plays an E-standard bass chart on guitar strings note-for-note; save +1 on a cello tuning to play bass charts an octave up by default.

Pick each string's open note, low to high — the BEADG bass and E standard guitar notes are marked for reference. Strings can repeat the same note (e.g. a unison pair); nothing requires them to be distinct. 4-8 strings, C-1 to E5.
